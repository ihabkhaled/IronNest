# 02 — Business Development

## Commercial Value

The change improves IronNest as a reusable delivery asset: adopters receive a
compiler and gate contract that is easier to trust and cheaper for humans and
AI agents to operate.

## Target Segment

Teams starting or hardening NestJS services with strict TypeScript, CI, and
AI-assisted delivery.

## Contract And SLA Impact

No customer API or runtime SLA changes. Build reproducibility and merge-gate
reliability improve.

## Rollout Audience And Enablement

All future IronNest consumers. README, toolchain context, skill routing, and
quality-gate docs are the enablement surfaces.

## Adoption Risks

Consumers with older local Node/npm versions will see engine warnings or cannot
prove clean-install parity until their runtime matches the pinned baseline.

## Exit Decision

Commercially justified as boilerplate reliability and maintenance-cost work.
